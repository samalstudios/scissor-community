The shortcuts below are written in the macOS form. On Windows and Linux, read ⌘ as Ctrl and ⌥ as Alt; ⇧ is Shift on every platform. Scissor shows whichever form matches the machine you are working on, so the menus on a Windows computer read Ctrl+S where this page reads ⌘S.

Shortcuts are matched by the physical position of the key rather than by the character it produces, so an AZERTY or Dvorak layout uses the same keys as a QWERTY one. Most shortcuts do nothing until a document is open; a handful - creating and opening documents, Preferences, the appearance settings and the Help items - work with no document on screen.

Menu items that are listed in Scissor but not yet working are shown greyed out and are left out of this reference, along with the shortcuts printed beside them.

Tools

Tool shortcuts are single keys pressed without a modifier, apart from those that take ⇧. They pick the tool straight away, including tools that sit inside a flyout behind another tool in the toolbar. Tools with no shortcut listed here - among them Group Selection, Area Type, Arc, Spiral, Polygon, Star, Smooth, Shear and Measure - are reached by holding the mouse on their toolbar slot and choosing from the flyout.

Fill, stroke and panels

These keys are not on any menu. They act on the fill and stroke swatches at the foot of the toolbar, and on the panels around the canvas.

ActionShortcut
Default fill and strokeD
Switch between the fill and the strokeX
Swap fill and stroke⇧X
Set the active swatch to None/
Hide or show all panelsTab

Whichever of the two swatches is active takes the next colour you choose. X moves the highlight between them, ⇧X exchanges the two colours, and / empties the active one.
