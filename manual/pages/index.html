Scissor is a free vector and pixel editor that runs entirely in your browser - nothing to install, no account, no upload. This manual covers every tool, panel and keyboard shortcut, the file formats it opens and writes, and how your work stays on your own device.

PrivacyNo server, no upload: your work stays with you.
